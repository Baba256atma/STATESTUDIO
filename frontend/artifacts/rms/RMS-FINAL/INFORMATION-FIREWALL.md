# Information firewall

A Ground Truth — sealed world, Observer-inspect only.

B Observable operational reality — Operator policy fields (`CAP_AV`, delayed inventory, missing downtime). `semanticConfirmation` stays false.

C Nexora knowledge — public session `nexoraKnowledge` has `groundTruthExposed: false` and empty facts. Conversation uses CC:5.

D Manager knowledge — profile/memory plus what Nexora told. No Ground Truth inspection.

Observer may read A–D and must not collapse them. `expectationKind` includes INFORMATION_BOUNDED.
