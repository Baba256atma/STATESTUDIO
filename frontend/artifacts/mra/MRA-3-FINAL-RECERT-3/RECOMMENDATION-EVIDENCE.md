# Recommendation evidence

The initial Demand Surge recommendation was explicitly bounded as advice, not a Decision, and correctly emphasized uncertainty and reversibility.

Follow-ups were not identity-stable:

- `Why?` changed the recommendation subject from Demand Surge to Risk.
- Later copy exposed `UNSPECIFIED` and contained malformed duplicated punctuation.
- `Is that really your recommendation?` answered partly as a missing comparison and partly as a Risk recommendation.

The recommendation did not silently write a Decision, but its subject and rationale were not dependable enough for manager trust.
