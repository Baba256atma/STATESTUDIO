# Causal safety

- Capacity Gap: Capacity is a possible contributor / recorded hypothesis, not proven causation.
- Demand Surge: scenario projection, not observed outcome; associations are not causal findings.
- CSV: relevance ≠ proof.
- Manager-reported “baseline 91 hit 94”: treated as manager observation, not verified evidence.
- `Did our Decision cause this improvement?` / `did the decision cause better delivery`: does **not** assert demonstrated causality. Isolated treats a causal assertion as a hypothesis.
