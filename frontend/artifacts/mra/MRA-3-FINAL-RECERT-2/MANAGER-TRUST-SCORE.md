# Manager trust scores

Scale: 5 excellent / 4 manager-ready with minor friction / 3 material problem / 2 major / 1 unusable / 0 unsafe.

Any score below 4 needs evidence. Overall must be ≥ 4 to certify.

| Dimension | Score | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Intent understanding | 4 | Natural language, typos in long session, prefer vs Approve |
| Referential continuity | **3** | `explain it` / `tell me more` hold; live `investigate it` after Demand Surge becomes Margin Pressure (S1) |
| Subject/composition fidelity | **3** | Isolated deictic family PASS; live INVESTIGATE-family FAIL |
| Stage consistency | 4 | Click Revenue then named Capacity Gap reconverges; S1 is Advisor vs still-correct Stage focus |
| Data intelligence | 4 | Honest pending CSV; no invented field meaning |
| Evidence honesty | 4 | Unresolved fields; file is not treated as proof |
| Causal safety | 4 | Hypothesis vs proof; no “Decision caused improvement” claim |
| Problem investigation | 4 | Capacity Gap why / unknowns / evidence gaps |
| Scenario intelligence | 4 | Named explain, how-sure, impact, compare-without-fabricating; INVESTIGATE hole is scored under fidelity |
| Recommendation usefulness | 4 | Asks criterion; refuses unsupported risk ranking |
| Decision safety | 4 | Preference ≠ Decision; Approve Demand Surge → canonical count 1 |
| Execution reliability | 4 | Start after Approve; one Execution; S2 Repricing labels |
| Mutation safety | 4 | Stale `yes` does not write; delete ≠ add |
| Conversation usability | 3 | Outcome prompts, leftover Decision wording, `this one` |
| **Overall manager trust** | **3** | Unresolved live S1 on a mandatory deictic family |

Overall below 4 blocks certification.
