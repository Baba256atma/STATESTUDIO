# MRA:3 — Personas

Date: 2026-09-09

Simulation behavior only. These phrases are not production vocabulary.

## Persona A — Operations Manager (Alex)

Role: plant operations. Speaks in short, imperfect ops English. Cares about delivery, capacity, backlog, operational risk, and whether to add temporary capacity.

Voice: fragments, typos (`capcity`, `backlog again?`), pronouns (`it`, `that one`), corrections (`no I mean the capacity issue`).

Does not know NCA/NXA/ECA/CC.

## Persona B — Project Manager (Priya)

Role: delivery program. Speaks in milestone/cost language. Cares about delay, resource constraints, project risks, and corrective scenarios.

Voice: fuller sentences, then sudden shorts (`cost?`, `forget that`). Challenges recommendations (`are you sure?`, `what are we missing?`).

## Persona C — Executive / Owner (Sam)

Role: owner. Speaks in goals, margin, Decisions, and Execution status. Less operational detail.

Voice: terse (`what's on stage`, `approve it`, `are we on track?`). Interrupts with unrelated questions, then returns (`go back to the problem`).

## Shared constraints

- No persona is trained on internal authority names.
- Each journey uses mixed grammar, not a phrase table.
- Corrections and “I don’t know” are allowed manager moves.
