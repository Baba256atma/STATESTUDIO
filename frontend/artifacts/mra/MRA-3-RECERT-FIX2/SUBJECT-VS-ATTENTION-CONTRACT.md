# Subject vs attention contract

Read-only invariant:

A valid explicit or resolved conversational subject owns a deictic investigation request unless the manager explicitly requests reprioritization or no reliable subject exists.

Example:

- Current subject: Demand Surge
- Manager: `investigate it`
- Therefore: `investigationSubject = Demand Surge`

Attention may still say Margin Pressure deserves higher executive attention. Those facts coexist. They are not one identity.

Attention target, top-priority Problem, intervention target, recommended Problem, and executive urgency are advisory. They are not automatically a referent authority for deictic operations.

Stage is context/attention evidence, not absolute conversational authority. Explicit `Investigate Margin Pressure` outranks Stage Demand Surge.
