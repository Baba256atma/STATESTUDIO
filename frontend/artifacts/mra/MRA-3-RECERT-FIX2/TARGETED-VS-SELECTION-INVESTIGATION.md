# Targeted vs selection investigation

These intents must not collapse.

## Targeted investigation

Utterances such as `investigate it`, `look deeper into it`, `what else do we know about it?`

Subject: established referent.

Classifier: `isTargetedDeicticInvestigationUtterance`.

CC:1 must not classify deictic `investigate it` as FOCUS.

## Investigation selection

Utterances such as `What should I investigate?`, `What should I look at first?`, `What needs my attention?`, `Actually, what should I investigate first?`

Subject: Nexora may choose using existing attention/urgency intelligence (often Margin Pressure on this catalog).

Classifier: `isInvestigationSelectionUtterance`.

## Explicit change

`Investigate Capacity Expansion Plan` / `Investigate Margin Pressure` / `Look deeper into Pricing Response` remain named FOCUS. Explicit manager reference outranks continuity.
