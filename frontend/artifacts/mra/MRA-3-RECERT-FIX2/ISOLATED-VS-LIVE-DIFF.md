# Isolated vs live

Identical sequence: `Demand Surge` then `investigate it`.

## Isolated CC:5 (before repair)

- Continuity active subject: Demand Surge
- Response: Demand Surge (RECERT-2 J-deictic did not reproduce the live steal)
- Recommendation overlay often used catalog evidence without live overview Advisor grounding ranking Margin Pressure into composition

## Live `/executive` (before repair)

- Stage / Manager–Object active object: Demand Surge (`ctx-scenario-demand`)
- Advisor grounding / attention: Margin Pressure (`ctx-problem-margin`)
- FOCUS + safe-action investigation used that attention as the composed investigation target
- Manager-facing answer: Margin Pressure

## Why live diverged

Live-only enrichment (Advisor overview grounding, attention subject, MO:6 recommended path, intervention ranking) is advisory. The pre-repair handoff treated FOCUS `investigate it` as permission to compose the recommended Problem.

Isolated suites that did not inject overview attention grounding therefore stayed on Demand Surge.

## After repair

Both isolated (with live-like `attentionSubjectId: ctx-problem-margin`) and live `/executive` must keep Demand Surge as the investigation/composition subject. Attention may still appear as related context when the manager asks Nexora to choose (`What should I investigate?`).
