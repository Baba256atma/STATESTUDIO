# Subject ownership contract

A newer valid manager-established subject owns subject-preserving follow-ups until a valid subject-changing operation occurs. Older Collection, Comparison, Stage, Attention, Recommendation, Advisory, and investigation state may remain supporting context but is not primary-eligible.

Subject-preserving operations include explain, tell more, targeted investigate/look deeper/what else, why, confidence, impact, evidence, uncertainty, and missing-information questions. Subject-changing operations include an explicit named or clicked object, ordinal selection, collection request, explicit comparison, explicit return, or attention/investigation selection request.

This contract is implemented through the existing canonical intent, continuity, Manager–Object, comparison, Stage, and composition authorities. No new persistence or business-truth authority exists.
