# Scenario remap protection

Scenario remap and assessment stay enabled.

Protected:

- Demand Surge → how sure are you? (epistemic / confidence)
- Demand Surge → why? (cause/assumption language on the Scenario)
- Demand Surge → what impact could it have?
- Show me scenarios → which one has the strongest evidence? (collection compare)

Blocked:

- Deictic follow-up remapping composition onto a **different** Scenario id (Capacity Expansion Plan while Demand Surge is the resolved subject).

`isScenarioAssessmentFollowUpOperation` still exempts how-sure/why/impact from stale **non-Scenario** capture. Different Scenario ids are not interchangeable composition subjects for deictic follow-ups.
