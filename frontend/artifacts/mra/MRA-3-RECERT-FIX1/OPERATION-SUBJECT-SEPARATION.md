# Operation vs subject

Operation (EXPLAIN, ELABORATE, INVESTIGATE, WHY, CONFIDENCE, IMPACT) is independent of subject (Demand Surge, Capacity Gap, …).

Changing operation must not silently change subject.

Example: Demand Surge then `explain it` vs `tell me more about it` vs `how sure are you?` may compose describe, elaboration, or epistemic assessment. All remain Demand Surge until the manager names another object (e.g. Tell me more about Capacity Expansion Plan).
