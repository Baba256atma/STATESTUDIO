# Clarification Policy

The Data Rail identifies material uncertainty; the existing Advisor asks the question. NCA:2 remains the sole pending-question state.

Selection rules:

1. ask only about fields linked plausibly to an existing supported executive metric;
2. prioritize conflicts, then ambiguous proposals, then likely proposals;
3. use file/domain context to prioritize the most relevant field;
4. ask one field question at a time;
5. do not ask about generic identifiers or descriptive columns merely because they are unknown;
6. keep ingestion possible when unrelated confirmed mappings are useful.

Natural answers supported: yes, an explicit yes-definition, correction, free natural definition, “I don't know,” and “ask me later.” Short answers resolve through the NCA pending field/source referent. Unknown/deferred answers leave the RDI semantic field unresolved without mutating Stage.

Manager correction outranks an unconfirmed proposal. If the correction has no proven canonical target, its scoped meaning is retained while the Data Reality mapper safely skips it.
