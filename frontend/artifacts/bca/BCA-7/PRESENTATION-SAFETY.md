# BCA:7 presentation safety

- Role relevance ≠ importance, recommendation, Stage membership, focus, or object size.
- Relationship copy uses “potentially related” / “cause has not been established”.
- Forecast remains forecast. General knowledge is not current failure.
- CEO/CFO role ≠ Decision authority. Presentation ≠ CC:10R / CC:11 mutation.
- Outcome copy does not attribute delivery improvement to a capacity action.
- Source and project refs do not leak.
- Manager-facing text must not contain BCA, NCA, DATA-ADV, sourceRef, or registry jargon.
