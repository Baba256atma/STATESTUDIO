# BCA:2 concept registry

The frozen initial registry is intentionally small. It proves extensibility without attempting a universal ontology.

Initial canonical concepts:

- Backlog
- Gross Margin
- Schedule Variance
- Milestone
- Cost
- Capacity
- On-Time Delivery
- Revenue
- Delivery
- Resource Availability
- Cost Variance
- Quality

Definitions contain exact aliases for already-resolved semantic phrases, a general meaning, applicable context kinds, ranked family relevance, safe non-causal associations, and rejected conclusions. No entry maps a raw CSV identifier such as BKL, OTD, or CAP_AV.

Registry growth is data-only: new definitions can be added without changing resolver logic. Full business/project ontologies, industry packs, external knowledge, and plugin systems remain out of scope.
