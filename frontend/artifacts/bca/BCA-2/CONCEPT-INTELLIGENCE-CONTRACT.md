# BCA:2 concept intelligence contract

`BusinessProjectConcept` records canonical meaning, BCA:1-qualified context kind, concept state/families/relevance, general and optional organization-specific meaning, evidence/provenance/confidence/confirmation, upstream ambiguity, safe associations, rejected inferences, `currentReality: NOT_ESTABLISHED`, and `executiveObjectId: null`.

Concept states are `KNOWN`, `PARTIALLY_UNDERSTOOD`, `MULTI_CONTEXT`, `AMBIGUOUS`, and `UNKNOWN`. Knowledge scope is independently `GENERAL` and/or `ORGANIZATION_SPECIFIC`.

```text
DATA-ADV established meaning + source authority
                         |
                         v
                 BCA:1 context
                         |
                         v
       exact established-meaning registry lookup
                         |
                         v
      immutable concept intelligence + diagnostics
```

Safety equations:

```text
Concept Knowledge != Business Reality
Contextual Association != Causality
Concept != Executive Object
Managerial Relevance != Decision Authority
```
