# NPA-T NMI:7 — Subject / referent boundary

NCA / existing referent authority resolves `it`.

NMI consumes `resolvedId`, NCA:2 `activeSubject`, or resolved name mapped onto the Management Map.

NMI does not independently parse a competing `it`.

Explicit named switch (Explain Margin Pressure) wins. Stale Attention/Map/roadmap/Stage/comparison IDs are ignored.
