# NPA-T NMI:7 — Activation rules

NMI overlay applies only when `nmiAdvisorBundle` is provided and `detectNmiAdvisorIntent` is not `NONE`.

Intents: LOCATION, RELATED, CAUSAL, ATTENTION, ROADMAP, NEXT, GAPS, EVIDENCE, KNOW.

Unrelated general conversation is unchanged (no bundle or NONE).

Locked mutation/commitment turns do not replace the existing Advisor answer.
