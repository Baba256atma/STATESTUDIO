# NPA-T NMI:FINAL — Decision Roadmap Audit

NMI:4 preserves:

- multiple roadmaps (Goal, Problem, Scenario, Decision, Execution anchors stay separate)
- branching Scenarios
- canonical convergence only
- statuses MISSING, PARTIAL, UNRESOLVED, NOT_REACHED, NOT_APPLICABLE

Separations:

- Scenario ≠ Decision
- Decision ≠ Execution
- Execution ≠ Outcome
- Outcome ≠ durable Learning

No roadmap stage manufactures the next stage. Advisor roadmap answers are descriptive (`roadmap position|descriptive`).
