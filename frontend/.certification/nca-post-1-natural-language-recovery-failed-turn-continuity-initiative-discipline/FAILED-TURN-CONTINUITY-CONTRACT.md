# Failed-turn continuity contract

`lastFailedTurn` on NCA:2 state records manager message, attempted reference, failure kind, and recoverability.

`why?` / `what do you mean?` after a failure explain the mapping failure, not an older business subject.

`forget it` closes repair without destroying the prior business thread. `go back to {subject}` restores that thread.

Successful high-confidence recovery never mentions the typo.
