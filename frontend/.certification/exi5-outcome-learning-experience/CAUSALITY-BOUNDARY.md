# EXI:5 — Causality Boundary

Causal language may only come from CORE-INT:3, consumed through CORE-OUT:2 causal Learning.

## Allowed without causal support

- “Capacity improved after the Decision.” (temporal association in Outcome text)
- “The expected Outcome was met. Causal attribution has not been established.”
- CORE-INT:3 `presentProvenAnswer` (“not proven”, possible contributors)

## Forbidden without supported CORE-INT:3 causal Learning

- “The Decision caused Capacity to improve.”
- “The Decision caused the improvement.”
- “Always use this Decision.”
- “This strategy works.”

EXI:5 sanitizes those phrases if they appear in projected text.

Outcome evaluation ≠ causal attribution.
